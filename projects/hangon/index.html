HangOn iOS Mobile App

Swift · iOS · Xcode · Kode With Klossy · August 2026

Swift iOS Xcode Keynote Kode With Klossy Apple Sustainability

HangOn is a sustainability and digital closet iOS app that helps users be more intentional about what they own, what they wear, and what they don't need to buy new. Selected as a finalist in the Kode With Klossy mobile app challenge and presented live at the Apple Store on Michigan Avenue in Chicago, Illinois.

Overview

01 — Inspiration

From Clueless to code

Inspired by Cher's iconic virtual closet from the Y2K film Clueless, HangOn brings that vision to life as a real, functional iOS app — built with a modern sustainability lens.

02 — Tech Stack

Swift & SwiftUI

Built natively in Swift using SwiftUI for the interface and Xcode as the development environment. A full-circle moment — Swift was one of the first languages I learned at Kode With Klossy.

03 — Sustainability

Intentional fashion

HangOn helps users track what they own, identify what they actually wear, and make more conscious decisions before buying new. The goal is to reduce fashion waste one closet at a time.

04 — Recognition

Apple Store finalist

Selected as a Kode With Klossy finalist and invited to present HangOn live at the Apple Store on Michigan Avenue in Chicago — in front of Apple staff, mentors, and a live audience.

Key Features

  • Digital closet to catalog and organize clothing items
  • Outfit builder to plan and save outfit combinations
  • Wear tracking to identify underused items in your wardrobe
  • Sustainability insights to encourage mindful shopping habits
  • Clean, intuitive SwiftUI interface designed for everyday use

Background

The idea for HangOn started years before this version existed. When I first learned to code, I wanted to build my own version of Cher's virtual closet from Clueless. In college, I built a prototype web app for a class project. When the Kode With Klossy mobile app challenge came around, I knew it was the moment to finally build the mobile version — the one I'd actually use every day.

Building it in Swift was a full-circle moment. Swift was one of the first languages I was ever introduced to, back at my very first Kode With Klossy camp. Coming back to it years later, building a full iOS app entirely on my own, felt like tangible proof of how far I've come since that first summer I learned what a variable was.

What's Next

HangOn is currently in active development. Future implementations include backend integration for cloud storage, user authentication, and social features — all with the goal of preparing it for a full App Store launch and real-world use by a broader audience.
